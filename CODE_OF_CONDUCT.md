# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Project participants are expected to maintain a respectful, inclusive, evidence-oriented environment. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and disclosure of private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Research and safety conduct

In addition to ordinary open-source conduct:

- protect participant privacy and never post identifiable health data;
- distinguish hypotheses, synthetic tests, engineering validation, and clinical evidence;
- disclose important limitations, conflicts, and uncertainty;
- avoid overstating therapeutic or medical claims;
- treat safety, allocation, outcome, and data-integrity concerns seriously;
- discuss technical disagreement through reproducible evidence and documented tradeoffs.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or the institutionally designated project contact. Do not use a public issue when a report contains personal, medical, legal, or security-sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, research integrity, privacy, or project safety. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, applied consistently, and minimize further disclosure of sensitive information.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers, reviews, project communication channels, and public representation of the project.
